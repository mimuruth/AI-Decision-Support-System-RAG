Investment Decision Memorandum
Series B Funding Round – TechCorp
===================================

Date: January 2025
Prepared by: Investment Committee
Classification: Strictly Confidential

1. Transaction Overview
-----------------------
TechCorp is seeking €50 million in Series B financing to fund:
  - Product development: €20 million (40%)
  - Geographic expansion into the US market: €18 million (36%)
  - Working capital and debt refinancing: €12 million (24%)

The current pre-money valuation is €180 million, implying a post-money
valuation of €230 million. This represents a 2.3x step-up from the Series A
valuation of €100 million in 2022.

2. Business Overview
--------------------
TechCorp develops AI-powered enterprise software solutions with a focus on
supply chain optimisation. Founded in 2019, the company has grown revenue
at a 45% CAGR over the past three years. Key metrics:
  - ARR: €28 million (as of Q3 2024)
  - Net Revenue Retention (NRR): 118%
  - Gross Margin: 72%
  - Burn Rate: €1.8 million per month

3. Investment Thesis
--------------------
The investment is predicated on three key pillars:

Pillar 1 – Market Opportunity:
The global supply chain optimisation software market is projected to reach
$45 billion by 2028 (CAGR: 11.4%). TechCorp's AI-native architecture positions
it well to capture share from legacy vendors who face significant technical debt.

Pillar 2 – Product Differentiation:
TechCorp's proprietary demand forecasting engine achieves 94% accuracy vs
the industry average of 82%. Patent protection covers three core algorithms.
The technology moat is considered sustainable for 3-5 years.

Pillar 3 – Management Team:
The founding team has two prior exits (one IPO, one strategic acquisition).
The recently appointed CFO brings public-company financial reporting experience.
The CTO is a recognised authority in applied machine learning.

5. Risk Assessment
------------------
The Investment Committee has identified the following material risks:

Risk 1 – Revenue Concentration (HIGH):
The top 3 clients represent 62% of total revenue. Loss of any single large
client would materially impair the financial projections. This risk is partially
mitigated by long-term contracts (average 2.3-year remaining term) and high
NRR indicating strong customer satisfaction.

Risk 2 – US Market Entry (MEDIUM-HIGH):
Entering the US market introduces execution risk, regulatory complexity, and
requires significant upfront investment. US competitors are well-capitalised.
Management's prior US experience partially mitigates this risk.

Risk 3 – Debt Refinancing (MEDIUM):
€85 million in debt maturing in Q3 2025 represents a near-term liquidity risk.
Proceeds from this Series B round provide a partial buffer. However, if
refinancing fails under unfavourable conditions, covenants may be triggered.

Risk 4 – Regulatory Compliance (MEDIUM):
Pending EU data privacy regulation compliance (75% complete) carries execution
risk. A €1.2 million budget shortfall has been identified. Non-compliance fines
could reach 4% of global turnover.

Risk 5 – Technology Disruption (LOW-MEDIUM):
Rapid advancement in AI technology could erode TechCorp's competitive advantage
faster than anticipated. The company must continuously invest in R&D to maintain
its accuracy advantage.

Aggregate Risk Score: 6.8/10 (MEDIUM-HIGH)

6. Valuation Analysis
---------------------
Comparable Company Analysis:
  - EV/ARR multiple (comparable SaaS peers): 6.0x – 9.0x
  - Implied valuation at 6.5x ARR: €182M (in line with ask)
  - Implied valuation at 8.0x ARR: €224M (above ask)

DCF Analysis (WACC: 18%, Terminal Growth: 5%):
  - Base Case NPV: €195M
  - Bear Case NPV: €130M (customer churn increases by 20%)
  - Bull Case NPV: €265M (US expansion outperforms by 30%)

Blended Valuation: €185M – slightly above current pre-money ask of €180M.
The investment appears fairly valued on a blended basis with upside optionality
from the US expansion.

7. Terms and Conditions
------------------------
- Investment: €50 million for 21.7% equity stake
- Preference: 1.5x non-participating liquidation preference
- Anti-dilution: Weighted average broad-based
- Board seats: 2 of 7
- Pro-rata rights: Full pro-rata in subsequent rounds
- Information rights: Monthly management accounts, annual audited financials

8. Committee Recommendation
-----------------------------
The Investment Committee recommends PROCEEDING with the investment subject to:
  1. Satisfactory completion of financial and legal due diligence
  2. Confirmation of debt refinancing progress with at least one term sheet
  3. Resolution of outstanding data privacy compliance matters
  4. Management sign-off on the US market entry plan with milestones

Confidence in Return: MEDIUM-HIGH
Expected Return Multiple: 3.5x – 5.0x over 5-year horizon
IRR Estimate: 28% – 38%
